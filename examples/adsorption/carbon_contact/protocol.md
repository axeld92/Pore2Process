# Carbon contact and segregation in the saved TRT packing

Reuse the balanced N96 carrier field and two repeated FCC cells. Track
65,536 inlet-flow-weighted inert markers with the archived 80 µs and
40 µs steps, molecular diffusivity and initial seed. Repeat at each
column-period Péclet number with the reference-geometry diffusivities
used in the preceding sensitivity. Keep the flow, wall and inlet/outlet
treatment of the qualified tracker. Exclude both exterior axial end
planes from the carbon contacts. The reconstructed solid is a set
of impermeable, nominally equal spherical carbon grain envelopes.

For each reflection at a carbon face, record twice the normal
overshoot in metres. The summed reflection length approximates
boundary local time. Raw hit counts are supplementary because they
depend on step size. Fold homologous faces onto one period and
attribute each reflection to its marker's elapsed transit segment.
Classify the first and second segments by their respective transit
time ranks and pair them by marker. Compute the total exposure share
by rank and the fraction of markers with any contact. Report the
observed transition out of the slowest tenth. Check the new tracking
against the archived transit pairs. Require at least 99.9% completed
pairs and verify contact event totals against each marker record.

Independently enumerate carbon faces and solid voxel volumes per grain
and octant from the saved geometry. Allocate each solid patch's mass to
ranks in proportion to their reflection length on its surface. Use
uniform particle-envelope density so that a patch's mass share is its
fraction of solid voxels. Scale to 7.032455 g with no inactive fraction.
Compare a grain-level aggregation and the direct total exposure share.
For the face-level diagnostic, weight each observed face once by area,
share that area among contacting classes, and report unresolved area
and overlap rather than treating unvisited faces as inaccessible.
These are contact-based mass allocations for sensitivity. The inert
trajectories can contact a shared carbon surface. They establish no
unique assignment of accessible pore capacity within a particle.

Check the integrated mean reflection length against 2*D*A_wall/Q
for a two-period uniform passive tracer. Use the saved voxel wall area
in that check. Allow 10% relative numerical or sampling difference.
Compare decile exposure and patch-mass allocations at 80 and 40 µs
within 3 percentage points per decile. Track time-step sensitivity
and finite sampling separately from any experimental uncertainty.
Compare the slow decile's estimated mass and surface sharing with
the preceding strong-enrichment and zero-exchange scenario. The
column geometry and contacts remain synthetic. No Figure 7 curve
or measured column response enters the estimates.
