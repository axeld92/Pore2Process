at 7.032455 g total dry carbon, using zero exchange, the original
synthetic law and a Péclet-matched law at each of three allocations.

the remaining delays fell to 7.98 and 4.16 min. That scenario passed
both curve RMSE screens and the 0.45 mm midpoint screen. The 0.80 mm
midpoint still failed. Both Figure 7 curves were scored after freezing
the predictions. Read-only replay verified 29 sources and 77 artifacts.
