# Carbon accessibility at fixed dry inventory

Run `20261117T090234Z-234da0ca` controls the 7.032455155 g dry
inventory and batch-qualified particles while varying only the
direct-contact fraction and external access resistance. All seven
scenarios for both sizes were frozen before the Figure 7 comparison.
Numerical refinement, phenol conservation and archived full-access
parity passed. Replay verified 27 sources and 31 artifacts.

Half the carbon isolated or communicating at 1% conductance gives
midpoints within 20% for both sizes but underpredicts their finite
window retention. Half communicating at 10% overpredicts retention
for both diameters. No scenario matches the midpoint and retained mass
together. [Report and reproduction](../../../examples/adsorption/carbon_access/REPORT.md)
