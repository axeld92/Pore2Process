geometry from the archived stock TRT N160 packing. The original
sections 1–2 set the reduced model's initial conditions and stationary
comparison. Fresh tracer probes supplied local exchange and time
moments. Original sections 3–8 supplied scoring data after the
predictions were frozen. All 65,536 fresh probes reached the outlet.

Maximum arrival-time variance errors were 9.03% and 5.93% for the
0.80 mm case at 80 and 40 µs. For 0.45 mm the errors were 12.23%
and 10.60%, exceeding the 10% limit. Mean arrival passed all four
cases. Read-only replay verified 36 sources and 142 artifacts.
This verification covers inert tracer only.
