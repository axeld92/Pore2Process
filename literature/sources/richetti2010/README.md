# Richetti et al. (2010): 2-ethylhexyl palmitate

[Article](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00449-009-0328-7), *Bioprocess and
Biosystems Engineering* 33, 331–337. User-supplied PDF in the private inbox,
hash in [metadata](metadata.json).

Figure 4 (printed p. 336) compares Novozym 435 at 70 °C, palmitic
acid:2-ethylhexanol 1:6 mol/mol and 150 rpm in a solvent-free orbital
shaker. Methods (p. 332) define wt% as mass of immobilised catalyst
relative to the combined initial masses of both substrates. No mixture
volume or density is given for this series. The authors report means of
triplicates with overall absolute conversion deviation around 5%
(p. 335), not individual replicate uncertainties.

[`loading_progress.csv`](loading_progress.csv) is a deliberately partial
digitization of the early progress (0–60 min), not the late near-complete
conversion plateau. Time is from the sampling schedule and plot marks.
The native 485 × 305 px Figure 4 image (PDF image Im8, page 6) was read
against the inset (x = 192.3–400.5 px for 0–1 h, y = 230.2–122 px for
0–100%) and main plot (y = 264–4.5 px for 0–100%). Conversions are
rounded to whole percentage points. The 15 and 20% marks overlap at
5 and 10 min; identical centers are reading scenarios, not measured
equality. The row margins are conservative digitization sensitivities
(larger for overlapping points), not experimental error bars. Remaining
overlaps at the 60 min plateau do not affect crossing targets ≤70%.
Use matched progress at 55, 60 and 70%, bracketed by positive-time
observations even at the highest loadings. The values of the printed
rate in the Figure 4 caption are separately transcribed in
[`initial_rates.csv`](initial_rates.csv); the paper labels them min⁻¹.
Use relative ratios; the initial slope estimator is not specified
sufficiently to treat these numbers as intrinsic molar rates.

Same catalyst preparation but different chemistry, feed ratio, temperature
and stirring. The authors propose agglomeration as a possible explanation;
no aggregate size or rheology measurement validates that mechanism.
