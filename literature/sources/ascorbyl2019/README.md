# Independent retained-activity evidence (2019)

[Synthesis with Immobilized Lipases and Downstream Processing of Ascorbyl Palmitate](https://doi.org/10.3390/molecules24183227),
*Molecules* 24, 3227. Section 2.2, Figure 1B and Table 1
report residual hydrolytic activity of Novozym 435 after
incubation in nonreactive organic solvents at 45 °C.
Acetone and acetonitrile cause a rapid loss of activity and a
subsequent plateau, with A/A0 = 0.20 and 0.24 after 144 h.
In 2-methyl-2-butanol, A/A0 remains 0.82 after 144 h,
following the initial decrease by 48 h.

This supports only the plausibility of an apparent activity
plateau following partial damage in the commercial preparation.
The solvents, exposure time, temperature and activity test
are different from neat pentyl propanoate esterification.
No retained fraction or time constant is transferred from
this source. The proposed f+(1-f)exp(-H) activity form is
a target diagnostic motivated also by the continuing late
conversion of the hot equimolar pentyl batches, not a
mechanistic identification of distinct enzyme populations.
