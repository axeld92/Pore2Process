# Paroul et al. (2010): geranyl propionate

[Article](https://doi.org/10.1002/jctb.2475), *Journal of Chemical
Technology and Biotechnology* 85, 1636–1641. User-supplied PDF in the
private inbox; see [metadata](metadata.json).

Figure 4 (p. 1640) compares Novozym 435 in solvent-free geraniol:propionic
acid 3:1 mol/mol at 50 °C, 150 rpm, with 4 Å molecular sieve
(20 mg/mL substrates from p. 1637). Catalyst loadings are relative
to total substrate mass (Methods, p. 1637). Mixture volume/density
is unreported. The kinetic results are triplicate means with an
overall absolute deviation around 5% conversion (p. 1639). The
experimental protocol states destructive runs; separate points
need not represent sequential samples from the same bottle.

[`loading_progress.csv`](loading_progress.csv) records rounded early
marker conversions from Figure 4 (native Im4, 385 × 280 px: 0 and
120% at y ≈ 244 and 4.5 px). The plotted first nonzero time is
0.25 h, even though the Methods list times starting at 0.5 h.
Retain the plotted time; zero is the assumed unreacted charge.
Use targets at 60, 70 and 80%, all reached after the first
positive-time observation in the primary series. Up to 2 h suffices
for these targets; omit the overlapping later plateau. Reading
margins of ±2 pp are digitization scenarios, not statistical errors.
Caption rates are transcribed separately in
[`initial_rates.csv`](initial_rates.csv).

**Between-series inconsistency:** Figure 3 (p. 1639, ratio series)
and Figure 5 (p. 1640, temperature series) both nominally include
the same 3:1 feed, 10 wt%, 50 °C and 150 rpm. Their black-square
series have visibly different conversions from Figure 4: at
0.5 h the three values are about 74%, 62% and 99%, respectively.
The Figure 3 initial rate at 3:1 is 1.48 min⁻¹ rather than Figure
4's 2.95 min⁻¹. Separate columns retain the alternate black-square
series as sensitivity cases; do not pool them as independent
replicates or choose the curve by any reactor score. The original
figures do not identify all run histories. Figure 5 prints a rate
for 40 °C only (not an exact 50 °C rate).
