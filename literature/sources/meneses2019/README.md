# Meneses et al. (2019): benzyl propionate

[Article](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00449-019-02159-w).
The author's [open thesis](https://repositorio.ufsc.br/handle/123456789/219184),
Chapter 3, printed pp. 47 and 50–51 (Figure 6), supplies the
methods and loading series. The catalyst-conditioning audit
also cites this study.

Novozym 435 at 5, 10 and 15 wt% relative to total fed
substrates, 50 °C, 150 rpm, molecular sieve. The run starts
with 35 mmol benzyl alcohol; 35 mmol propionic acid is
metered in six portions through the first five hours.
The nominal catalyst/initial-liquid ratio changes during
feeding. The approximate conversions at 5 h are 17, 40
and 68%; at 8 h they are 30, 48 and 89%. The lowest
loading reaches only about 40% even by 36 h, and the highest
exceeds 60% when feed finishes. There is no common post-feed
conversion interval across all three loads. Do not apply
a same-initial-state time-rescaling test to these fed-batch
curves: acid addition and water removal alter the state.
The study is relevant to acid inhibition and catalyst
conditioning, not an independent high-solids kinetic law
for the current fixed-feed reactor.
