# Batch progress curves for the 2:1 propanoate reaction

The [2025 supporting information](https://acs.figshare.com/articles/journal_contribution/_/29631736)
contains batch experiments at 40–80 °C for the same acid, alcohol and catalyst
as the packed-bed experiment. Table S2 labels the relevant 10% catalyst runs
19 and 21–24. It gives 25.3 g/L of immobilised preparation, 3.3 mol/L acid,
and an initial alcohol-to-acid ratio of 2. The printed catalyst concentration
and feed concentration imply 7.6667 g of catalyst per mole of acid. The
nominal 10% mass ratio implies 7.40785 g/mol at 74.0785 g/mol acid.
Keep these two printed bases distinct.

The Figure S3 caption reverses panels (b) and (c). Panel (c) itself says
pentyl propanoate and ratio 2, and its left concentration axis is consistent
with the lower feed concentration. Panel (b) itself says ratio 1 and has
the higher concentration scale. The
[digitized observations](batch_2to1_markers.csv) use the filled markers
in panel (c), as labelled inside the figure. The right-hand conversion
axis is used directly. The dual left axis increases from zero and its
full scale is about 3.4 mol/L, whereas Table S2 prints an inlet of
3.3 mol/L. The kinetic calculation uses the Table S2 feed concentration.

On page 9 rendered at 190 dpi, the horizontal axis runs from x = 875.5
to 1313.5 pixels over 0 to 10 h. The right axis runs from y = 1376
to 938 pixels over 0 to 100% conversion. Time in the CSV is the mapped
value in minutes, rounded to 0.1 min, and conversion is a fraction
rounded to 0.001. The markers were identified by color and extent
and checked visually on a 380 dpi crop. Each temperature also has
the shared initial condition at time zero, with no individual marker
coordinate. The table has 68 selected post-start markers. Some
overlapping colors could not be read separately. Two pixels correspond
to about 2.74 min and 0.46 percentage points on these axes. This
describes digitization scale and carries no statistical uncertainty.

The independence of the 2025 and 2026 assays remains unknown. The papers
report their results in different forms. These records belong to the
2025 experimental program that supplied the authors' kinetic calibration.
